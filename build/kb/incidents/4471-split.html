The three problems

parser: dates read month first. The shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 2 March becomes 3 February. Comes in through the app, by email and through booking-v2’s form. This is the fix that is queued.

hint: booking-v3’s date hint says MM/DD/YYYY. booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, but reads what people type the tenant’s way, day first. People follow the hint, so their dates flip even after the parser is fixed. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The fix is in the form: the hint must match the tenant’s locale.

shift: the date moves by one day. These reports are not a swap at all: Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made, a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto got different days for the same meeting, every booking in June is one day off. The desk already noted them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), then filed them under parser by tenant. It looks like a time-zone or daylight-saving conversion, and the parser fix will not touch it.

How the reports were placed

  • One report per id: the export pages overlap, so repeats are counted once (uniqueById).
  • The tenant decides, not intake’s tag: meta.intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant often.
  • A report on the desk’s one-day-move list is shift; every other report gets its cause from causes.ts (tenant locale and booking form).
